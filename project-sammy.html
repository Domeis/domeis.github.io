Brain Coach Sammy : Digital Assessment and Training for Cognitive Impairment

R&D & Product Lead • HAII Corp. • Digital health

Overview

In this post, I would like to introduce Brain Coach Sammy. My team and I worked with clinicians from Ewha Woman's University Hospital to develop cognitive training programs for the elderly. This solution focuses on delaying the cognitive decay due to Alzheimer's and Related Diseases (ADRD) and preventing it through cognitive engagement. I'm particularly proud of this product because the amount of work and attention our team have put in and how it has received by the users.

Sammy: The Brain Coach

Beyond diagnostics, Sammy the Brain Coach serves as the therapeutic treatment solution for cognitive impairment. Sammy was designated as an innovative product by the Public Procurement Service.

The core of Sammy is in that users complete a 10 minute cognitive assessment, then based on the results, we personalize the difficulty of the training programs. Users repeat the assessment every 28 days to track their cognitive change. There are currently total of 15 training programs that target memory, executive function, attention, visuospatial, language, and calculation.

UX Challenges for Elderly Users

Sammy was originally a chatbot within KakaoTalk, the most popular messenger app in South Korea. However, designing effective training programs within the messenger was limited (i.e., using GIFs to display tasks, offering only pre-determined choices, and requiring cumbersome alphanumeric switching on native keyboards). So our team rebuilt the application so that it could fully embrace the cognitive training program as well as providing tailored UX for the elderly.

The Research

"TL;DR: Those with mild cognitive impairment showed significant positive changes in cognitive scores compared to the control group in CERAD-K."

Our team conducted a 12 week study at Suncheon City Dementia Care Center, a public facility established in local health centers to provide comprehensive support for dementia prevention, early screening, and care for patients and their families.
A total of 125 older adults completed the study. We classified cognitive status using the Clinical Dementia Rating (CDR): 0 = Cognitively normal (CN), 0.5 = Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI), 0.5 ~ 2 = Mild Dementia. Participants who were assigned to the intervention group used Sammy, while control group received usual care from the center.


Our results showed that participants with MCI showed significant positive cognitive change than the control group with a moderate effect size. In contrast, participants who were cognitively normal or had mild dementia showed no significant differences between the two groups.

This advantage became even stronger when adjusting for equal intervention duration. Under this comparison, the cognitive benefit was heavily concentrated in the MCI cohort, where participants using Sammy demonstrated substantial improvements over usual care with a large effect size. These findings highlight that Sammy is particularly effective as a targeted intervention during the critical early window of mild cognitive impairment.

Implementation to Samsung SmartTV

Samsung approached us with an opportunity to integrate Brain Coach Sammy across Samsung Smart TVs worldwide. To meet the milestone of showcasing the product live at CES 2026, we formed a dedicated task force to transition our mobile application into a livingroom experience.

Designing for a TV interface required rethinking our interaction paradigm. Direct touch and swipe gestures had to be replaced entirely by a standard TV remote control, where complex navigation or rapid button presses would cause friction. We audited our training program, redesigning exercises around the directional D-pad and removing touch-dependent mechanics that did not translate naturally to a remote.

To increase engagement, we also implemented a gamification element called "brain age" based on the cognitive assessment. Our aim with brain age was to alleviate the anxiety often tied to test scores by providing users with a metric that felt more intuitive, encouraging, and actionable.

Our efforts were met with successful launch of Sammy, receiving media attention worldwide. We continue to further develop Sammy in hopes of acheiving the company mission: one more healthy day for millions.
